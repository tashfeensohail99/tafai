Document Detection & Verification System 
The client portal will include an intelligent document management system that can: 
• Detect which required documents are uploaded  
• Detect which required documents are missing  
• Identify missing client data/information  
• Sort uploaded documents into correct categories  
• Check document expiry dates  
• Mark expired or near-expiry documents  
• Flag unclear, duplicate, wrong, or incomplete documents  
• Notify employee/admin when documents need review  
Missing Documents & Missing Data Retrieval 
When the system detects that a client has not provided required documents or required 
information, it will automatically generate follow-up messages. 
The system will generate: 
• WhatsApp message to client  
• Email to client  
• Employee task/reminder  
• Client portal notification  
Example: 
Dear [Client Name], 
Your application is pending because the following items are missing: 
1. Passport copy 
2. CNIC front side 
3. Employment experience letter 
4. Updated phone number 
Please upload the missing documents through your client portal or send them on WhatsApp.

23. Employee Attendance System 
Clean name: AI Face-Based Employee Attendance & Time Tracking System 
Explanation: 
This module will allow the company to track employee attendance using face detection / face 
verification. Employees will mark attendance from the company mobile app or office device. 
The system will verify the employee’s face, record check-in/check-out time, and generate 
attendance reports for payroll and management. 
This module should be linked with the Payroll System, Employee Monitoring System, and Daily 
Operations Reporting System. 
What we will build: 
• Employee face registration/enrollment  
• Face-based check-in  
• Face-based check-out  
• Attendance dashboard  
• Late arrival tracking  
• Early leave tracking  
• Break time tracking  
• Overtime tracking  
• Leave/absence tracking  
• Location-based attendance validation  
• Device-based attendance validation  
• Attendance approval/rejection by admin  
• Monthly attendance reports  
• Payroll attendance sync  
• Suspicious attendance alerts

System verifies face 
↓

↓ 
Attendance is marked

The system can support:
