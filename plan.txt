## Plan: Tashfeen AI Platform Master Delivery Plan

This is the corrected master delivery plan for the AI-Powered Overseas Consultancy Management & Automation Platform. It uses `D:\tafsheen\ai.txt` as the technical bible and `D:\tafsheen\agreement.txt` as the delivery, acceptance, and legal frame, with user-confirmed commercial correction that the final project amount is CAD 10,500. Target workspace file for execution handoff: `D:\tafsheen\plan.txt`.

**Correction Notes Before Final Use**
- Final project amount: CAD 10,500 software development only.
- Previous USD 8,000 references are replaced by CAD 10,500.
- Blank legal fields in the agreement are intentionally not filled in this planning document.
- Flutter work is moved earlier: planning/API contract starts Week 2, Flutter shell starts Week 4, active mobile development starts Week 9, and Week 15 becomes completion/stabilization rather than first start.
- Reporting and audit foundation starts Week 2, with usable management reporting expanded through Weeks 3, 4, 12, and 14.
- Phase 2 is clearly marked as basic only throughout this plan.
- Strong dependency disclaimers are included for WhatsApp, telephony, AI, app stores, and social media APIs.
- Post-handover support severity rules are included.

**Plan Boundaries**
- Duration: 16 active delivery weeks plus 8 grace/stabilization weeks.
- Agreement amount: CAD 10,500 software development only.
- Third-party costs: client pays separately and are not included in CAD 10,500.
- Mobile stack: Flutter for Android and iOS, as required by agreement.
- Web stack: Next.js/React/TypeScript for admin, employee, partner/referral, and client portals.
- Backend stack: NestJS/Node.js modular monolith for CRM, RBAC, workflows, integrations, APIs.
- AI worker stack: Python FastAPI for OCR, speech-to-text, AI summaries, document classification, interviews, and business plan generation.
- Database/storage: PostgreSQL, Redis/BullMQ, S3-compatible object storage.
- AI rule: AI assists only; human staff must review all legal, immigration, eligibility, document, financial, interview, call summary, and business plan outputs.
- Call rule: recordable calls require SIP/telephony provider support and consent policy; WhatsApp app calls are not treated as reliably recordable.
- Phase 2 rule: Month 4 Phase 2 items are basic rollout only, not full enterprise-grade MDM, ERP, biometric, endpoint security, HRMS, accounting, payroll, or device-control software.
- Legal-field rule: blank legal fields in the agreement should be completed by authorized parties separately if needed; this plan does not fill legal blanks.

**Security And Privacy Architecture**
- Authentication: secure login for admin, employee, partner, and client users; hashed passwords; password reset; session timeout; MFA-ready design for future activation.
- Authorization: server-side RBAC for every route and API; no frontend-only permission enforcement; department-level and ownership-level record scoping.
- Data isolation: clients see only their own cases/documents; partners see only limited referral data; employees see only assigned/department-permitted data.
- Audit logging: record logins, failed logins, user creation, role changes, lead assignment, status changes, document access, payment changes, exports, handovers, integration events.
- File privacy: documents stored in private object storage; access through signed URLs; no public document links; virus scanning/provider scanning if available.
- PII handling: collect only necessary immigration/client data; restrict sensitive fields; use consent and privacy acknowledgement where needed.
- AI privacy: do not send unnecessary PII to AI providers; mask or reduce data where possible; store prompt/output logs only where needed for audit and improvement.
- Call/voice privacy: call recording and transcription require provider support and client/employee consent policy.
- Employee monitoring privacy: attendance, device registry, activity monitoring, and any face/GPS/device checks require disclosed policy and consent.
- Backup privacy: backups encrypted or provider-secured; access limited to authorized technical administrators.
- Incident handling: define who is notified, severity, containment, audit review, and fix timeline.

**DevOps, Deployment, Backup, And Environments**

- Deployment: Dockerized backend, web app, AI worker, queue worker; managed PostgreSQL where possible; Redis; object storage bucket.
- CI/CD: automated build, lint, test, migration check, deployment to staging, manual approval for production.
- Secrets: environment variables/secrets manager; no secrets in source code; separate staging and production credentials.
- Database migrations: reviewed migration files; backup before production migration; rollback plan for high-risk changes.

- Monitoring: application logs, error alerts, API health checks, queue failure alerts, disk/storage monitoring, integration webhook failure alerts.

**Formal Change Request Workflow**
- Step 1: client submits change request in writing by email/WhatsApp/document, describing desired change, reason, users affected, urgency, and expected output.
- Step 2: development team classifies it as bug, clarification, small adjustment within approved scope, or new change request.
- Step 3: for change requests, team prepares impact note covering cost, timeline, affected modules, third-party dependency, testing effort, and risk.
- Step 4: client approves or rejects impact note in writing before work starts.
- Step 5: approved change is added to backlog with separate acceptance criteria and delivery date.
- Step 6: unapproved requests are not included in current milestones.
- Bug definition: agreed feature does not work as accepted or described.
- Change request definition: new feature, new workflow, new screen, major wording/business logic change, new integration, new report/export, advanced Phase 2 capability, or redesign after approval.

**Post-Handover Support Severity Rules**
- Support period: 90 days free bug-fix support after final handover, as defined in agreement.
- Severity 1 critical: production system unavailable for all users, login down for all users, severe data access/security issue, or payment/document upload completely blocked. Target response: same business day where possible. Target workaround/fix: urgent patch or rollback depending on cause.
- Severity 2 high: major workflow blocked for a department, integration failing where provider is operational, major report/payment/case status error. Target response: 1 business day. Target fix/workaround: 2-3 business days depending on complexity.
- Severity 3 medium: non-critical bug with workaround, UI issue affecting work speed, incorrect label/status wording, partial report mismatch. Target response: 2 business days. Target fix: next maintenance patch.
- Severity 4 low: cosmetic issue, minor text change, non-blocking improvement, training/help clarification. Target response: 3-5 business days. Target fix: scheduled patch if within bug scope.
- Exclusions: new features, new modules, new integrations, design redesign, business logic changes, third-party downtime, third-party billing, provider policy changes, client-side operational mistakes, data entry mistakes, AI usage charges, and post-handover enhancements.
- Support process: client reports issue with screen name, user role, steps to reproduce, expected result, actual result, screenshot/video if possible, and sample record ID.
